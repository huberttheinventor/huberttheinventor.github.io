What is collected, and what is not.

Nothing is collected from you at the moment. There is no signup form, no analytics and no cookies on these pages. What follows describes that, and what will change when the mailing list opens.

Nº001 / What is collected

Nothing, today

The mailing list is not open yet, so there is no form to type an address into. Nothing on these pages requires an account, and nothing is recorded about readers who simply read.

Nº002 / Analytics

None, and no cookie banner

These pages run no analytics, set no cookies, and load nothing from an advertising network — which is why there is no cookie banner. If counting is added later it will be GoatCounter, chosen because it sets no cookies and stores neither personal data nor full IP addresses, and this page will say so before it is switched on.

Nº003 / When the list opens

One address, for one purpose

Your email address will be the only personal thing collected, and only if you type it into the form and tick the consent box. It will be used to send new field guides and nothing else — not sold, rented, traded, or shared with advertisers, and not used to build a profile of you. The list will run on Buttondown, which would store the addresses and send the emails as the processor, under their privacy policy. This page will be updated to describe that as fact, not as a plan, before any address is collected.

Nº004 / Your rights

Once there is anything to exercise them on

  • Unsubscribe — every email will carry a working unsubscribe link, and using it deletes you from the list.
  • Access or deletion — ask, and your address is removed along with any record of it.
  • Correction — reply to any newsletter to change your address.

If you are in the EU or UK, these are your rights under the GDPR. The lawful basis for holding your address would be your consent, given by ticking the box on the form, and you could withdraw it at any time by unsubscribing. Today none of this is engaged, because no address has been asked for.

Nº005 / Retention

How long it would be kept

Until you unsubscribe. There is no archive of removed subscribers.
