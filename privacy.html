What is collected, and what is not.

One thing is collected, and only if you hand it over: an email address, typed into the form at the foot of a guide. There are no analytics, no cookies and no tracking on these pages. What follows says exactly what happens to that address.

Nº001 / What is collected

One address, and only if you type it

Every field guide ends with a form for the mailing list. If you fill it in and tick the consent box, your email address is collected — and that is the only personal thing this site ever asks for. Nothing requires an account, and nothing at all is recorded about readers who simply read and leave.

Each form carries a tag naming the guide it sits on, so the list knows which guide brought you. That tag is attached to your subscription, not to any record of your browsing.

Nº002 / Analytics

None, and no cookie banner

These pages run no analytics, set no cookies, and load nothing from an advertising network — which is why there is no cookie banner. If counting is added later it will be GoatCounter, chosen because it sets no cookies and stores neither personal data nor full IP addresses, and this page will say so before it is switched on.

Nº003 / Browser storage

Two settings, kept on your own device

Not cookies, and not sent anywhere: two values are written to this site’s local storage in your browser, and neither leaves your device. hubert:reading remembers whether you chose the paper or the panel reading plate, so the site does not forget between pages. hubert:motion records whether the animation layer started correctly, which is used to keep the text visible if it did not. hubert:list counts how many times you have seen the mailing list dialog since you last dismissed it, so it does not show on every single visit — and if you join the list, it stops asking for good. Clearing your browser’s site data removes all three, and the site works normally without them.

Nº004 / The mailing list

One address, for one purpose

Your email address is the only personal thing collected, and only if you type it into the form and tick the consent box. It is used to send you the eight existing guides on confirmation, then one new guide a week, and nothing else — not sold, rented, traded, or shared with advertisers, and not used to build a profile of you.

The list runs on Buttondown, which stores the addresses and sends the emails as the processor, under their privacy policy. Signup is confirmed: Buttondown emails you first, and you are not on the list and receive nothing until you click the link in that email. If you never click it, the address is never activated.

Nº005 / Your rights

How to exercise them

  • Unsubscribe — every email carries a working unsubscribe link, and using it deletes you from the list.
  • Access or deletion — ask, and your address is removed along with any record of it.
  • Correction — reply to any newsletter to change your address.

If you are in the EU or UK, these are your rights under the GDPR. The lawful basis for holding your address is your consent, given by ticking the box on the form and confirmed by clicking the link in the confirmation email, and you can withdraw it at any time by unsubscribing.

Nº006 / Retention

How long it is kept

Until you unsubscribe. There is no archive of removed subscribers.
