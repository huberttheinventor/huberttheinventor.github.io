# Nº005 — Zero bars. Still found.

Full narration, by section. This is what is spoken in the film, word for word.

*Independent field guide. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to

## Hook

Good news, everyone! Your telephone can find itself with zero bars, no
internet, and no help from anyone.

## 01 · Who's up there

So there's a satellite up there... looking at me? Looking at you! Hah! The
satellite has no idea you exist, and it never will.

There is a whole fleet of the things in orbit, and each one does precisely
one job: it shouts the time. Constantly. Into the void.

## 02 · What it actually sends

The time? That's it? My microwave does that. Your microwave is not bolted to
an atomic clock, Fry. Every shout says: this is which satellite I am, and
this is exactly when I said it.

And my phone... answers? Never! Not once! Your phone only listens. It is the
finest eavesdropper ever built.

## 03 · The gap is the distance

The shout takes time to arrive. Your phone compares when it was sent to when
it landed — and that little gap is the distance.

One satellite, one distance. Which puts you somewhere on a sphere. A second
narrows it, a third pins it, and a fourth corrects your phone's own sloppy
clock. There you are.

## 04 · Why it wants wifi anyway

Then why does it ever want the internet? Only to hurry! The network tells
your phone which satellites are overhead so you aren't left standing in a
car park waiting for the sky to introduce itself.

## Payoff

Bars are a conversation. This is a sermon. You are merely in the
congregation. I'm in a congregation of space clocks. And you still cannot
find your keys.
