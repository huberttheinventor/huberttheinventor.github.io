# Nº003 — Five machines carry your message. None can read it.

## Hook

Your message passes through five different machines before it reaches your
friend, and not one of them can read a word of it. Good news, everyone! I've
finally built something that keeps a secret better than I do.

## 01 · The open line

The first machine is the one your phone never lets go of. A single line, held
open for hours, doing nothing at all. It never asks what you're saying. It only
asks where it goes.

## 02 · The locked box

Because before your message leaves your phone, it's locked, and the only keys
are on your device and your friend's. So machine two, the one holding your
message while your friend's phone lies face-down in a drawer, is a warehouse
full of boxes it cannot open.

## 03 · The other three

That photo you sent never went down the line at all. It went to machine three, a
separate store, locked the same way. Machine four taps your friend's phone on
the shoulder to wake it up, and isn't told why. And machine five, the little one
whispering "typing", knows only that a thumb is moving.

## 04 · Payoff

So that's the whole arrangement: five machines, all of them couriers, not one of
them a reader. The only thing that ever reads your message is the phone in your
friend's hand. Hand over everything the company has and what you get is the
envelope — although, and do let me finish, the envelope says rather a lot.
