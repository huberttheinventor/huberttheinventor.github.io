# Nº004 — It doesn't search the internet. It searches a copy.

Full narration, by section. This is what is spoken in the film, word for word.

*Independent field guide. Not affiliated with Google LLC. It carries no figures
for index size, query volume or latency, because none is needed to explain it.*

## Hook

Good news, everyone! When you search Google, it doesn't look at the Internet.
It looks at a copy it already made. Somebody finally solved reading
everything, and it wasn't me.

## 01 · The machine that reads

One machine has been out there for years reading. It fetches a page, takes it
apart, and writes down every word and where it found it, not the page itself.
A list of which words are on which pages.

## 02 · The copy is the whole trick

So by the time you type anything, the work is already done. Your question
never goes out to the Internet at all. It goes to that list. That's why the
answer arrives in less time than it takes you to lift your finger off the key.

## 03 · The machine that guesses

But a list of words isn't an answer because you don't type what you mean. You
type four words with a spelling mistake in one of them. The second machine's
entire job is working out what you actually meant, and it has never fetched a
page in its life.

## 04 · Two of them

So there are two of them. One read everything and understands nothing. The
other understands you and has read nothing. Neither can do the other's job.
Both have to be right, and they have to finish before you get bored and leave.
