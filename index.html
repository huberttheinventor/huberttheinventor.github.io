Nº001 / Intro

Most explanations of a large system are either a press release or a lecture. The press release tells you nothing and the lecture assumes you already know. Neither one leaves you able to picture the thing — which is the only part that was ever worth having.

There is a better way.

These are field guides to machines you use every day and nobody explains. One system per guide, taken apart on screen: what it is made of, which decisions sit inside it, and why those decisions were made that way and not another way.

Everything is drawn rather than stocked, and everything is built from primary sources you can go and read yourself. No thread-bait, no “this changes everything”, and no number I cannot show you the source for. If a claim cannot be checked, it does not go in.

The first guide is on the machine that moves more video than anything else on Earth.

Nº003 / Method

One system per guide, taken apart until it fits in your head.

Nº001

One mental model

A guide carries one idea and everything in it earns its place against that idea. If a section is interesting but belongs to a different model, it is cut and saved for the guide where it fits.

Nº002

Primary sources or nothing

Engineering blogs, papers and specifications written by the people who built the thing. Never a summary of a summary, and never a figure carried in from memory. The reading list at the end of each guide is the working bibliography, not decoration.

Nº003

Drawn, not stocked

Every diagram is built for the guide it appears in. Nothing is a stock illustration of a server rack, because a stock illustration of a server rack has never explained anything to anyone.

Nº004

Orders of magnitude

Where a real number is computed per case and would be wrong stated precisely, the guide gives the order of magnitude and says so. A confident-looking table of exact figures is the fastest way to be confidently wrong.

Nº005

No pitch

Nothing here is selling anything. There is a list, because platforms are rented and an audience is not, and it carries the guides and nothing else — with an unsubscribe link that works.
