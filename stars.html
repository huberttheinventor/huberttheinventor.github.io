STARS / THE SIX-STAR CARD

The six-star card: how GTA VI police keep a file on you

In GTA V, losing the police ended it. In GTA VI, the police keep notes: the wanted stars go up to six, a row of icons under them shows what the police know about you, and the colour of a star says where you stand. This card lists only what was reported before and after release. Everything here comes from press coverage and player guides; no Rockstar document was read, so none of it is an official rulebook.

1. Six stars

GTA VI has six wanted stars. GTA V stopped at five. (allthings.how; Push Square)

2. How a crime gets reported

A crime is reported when a witness sees it, an alarm goes off or a vehicle tracker reports it. (Beebom; witness and alarm also Tech4Gamers)

3. What each star state means

  1. Hollow: a crime was reported, and you are not identified.
  2. Solid white: the police can see you.
  3. Flashing white: they lost sight of you and are searching.
  4. Red: you escaped the chase, but the crime scene is still a hot zone.

(Beebom; Tech4Gamers; hollow and flashing also GTABase)

4. The icons under the stars: what the police know

A row of icons under the stars shows what the police know about you:

  1. Camera (CCTV) footage.
  2. Your face or description.
  3. Your outfit.
  4. Your vehicle.
  5. A visible weapon.
  6. That they are looking for a pair.

(Beebom; Dexerto; GTABase. Rockstar North's Rob Nelson is quoted by Beebom as confirming the police can know your appearance, clothing and vehicle.)

5. The hot zone

The area around the crime, or around your last known location, stays hot. It shows as a red circle on the map where the police keep searching, even after the stars are gone. At six stars the search is map-wide. (Beebom; Tech4Gamers; Dexerto; GTABase)

6. Getting spotted again

If you are spotted in the hot zone, or commit another crime there, the stars come back. (Beebom; GTABase)

7. How to cut what they know

  • Wear a mask before the crime.
  • Change your outfit.
  • Switch to a different car, or leave the old one.
  • Split Jason and Lucia up.
  • Leave the area and lie low.

(Beebom; GTABase; Dexerto. The outfit point also comes from Nelson, as quoted in GAMES.GG's report.)

8. What four stars looked like in the Extended Look

In the chase shown in the Extended Look, four stars brought cop cars, SUVs and helicopters. This comes from a single outlet (allthings.how), describing that one scene, so do not read it as a full list of what each star level sends. (allthings.how)

Remember it like this

Losing the chase is the quick part. Getting the police to forget you is the slow part.
